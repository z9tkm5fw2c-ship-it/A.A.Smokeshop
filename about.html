A neighborhood shop, not a chain

A A Smoke Shop sits on Old Spanish Trail in Houston, stocked and run by people who use this stuff themselves.

We opened A A Smoke Shop to be the kind of place you can walk into with a question and walk out with an actual answer — not a shrug.

Houston has no shortage of smoke shops. What we're going for is the one where the person behind the counter can tell you the difference between two mods, point you to a piece that fits your budget, or tell you straight that something isn't worth your money.

That means keeping a tight, well-chosen selection rather than a shelf crammed with everything — and keeping it fresh as what's good changes.
